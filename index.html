01 / About

Researcher, engineer, educator

I'm an ML researcher and engineer, currently doing the Master in Artificial Intelligence (MAI) in Barcelona, a joint program of UB, UPC and URV. My main interests are deep learning and world models, which I write about on my blog.

Before that, I spent more than two years as an ML R&D Engineer at Deep Origin, working on AI for drug discovery. I developed DOScore and other scoring models for virtual screening, which achieve state-of-the-art performance on benchmarks such as DUD-E, DEKOIS 2.0, Merck and JACS, and Togo, a foundation model for molecular property prediction that achieves state-of-the-art performance on public benchmarks such as TDC.

I hold a BSc in Applied Statistics and Data Science from Yerevan State University. Outside research, I co-founded the Armenian Robotics Alliance, where I run robotics programs and national competitions for students.
